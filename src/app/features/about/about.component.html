Our Story

Where Learning Inspires Growth

Established in 2073 BS, Pragyan Paathshala is committed to creating a joyful, meaningful, practical and child-friendly learning environment where every child is encouraged to discover their potential and grow with confidence.

Located in Latikoili, Birendranagar-9, Surkhet, Pragyan Paathshala believes that education is more than acquiring knowledge from textbooks. It is about nurturing curiosity, creativity, discipline, compassion, critical thinking and the confidence to face life's challenges.

With a student-centered approach to teaching and learning, we provide educational opportunities that support the intellectual, emotional, social, physical and creative development of every student. From the foundational years of early childhood education to Grade 10, we are dedicated to guiding students through every stage of their educational journey.

At Pragyan Paathshala, we strive to create an environment where students feel valued, motivated, supported and inspired to become responsible individuals and capable citizens of society.

Purpose & Direction

Our Vision and Mission

Our Vision

To become a model school with a strong sense of social responsibility, committed to identifying and nurturing the inherent talents of every child, empowering them to make the most of opportunities for personal growth, and inspiring them to become capable, responsible and good citizens of society and the nation.

Our Mission

To provide quality education through a child-friendly, inclusive and engaging learning environment that encourages students to learn according to their interests, abilities and individual needs.

We aim to promote meaningful learning experiences, develop essential life skills, nurture creativity and curiosity, encourage discipline and self-reliance, and foster respect, empathy, cooperation and social responsibility in every student.

Our Educational Journey

A Journey of Learning from Early Childhood to Grade 10

Pragyan Paathshala provides a continuous learning journey from early childhood education through Grade 10, supporting students as they grow from curious young learners into confident, responsible and independent individuals.

Our educational journey is designed to build strong foundations in the early years, strengthen academic understanding in the middle grades, and prepare students for the academic challenges, responsibilities and opportunities of secondary education.

Through supportive teachers, engaging learning activities, academic guidance and a nurturing environment, we encourage our students to develop the knowledge, skills, confidence and values they need for the next chapter of their lives.

Teaching & Learning Approach

Learning Beyond the Classroom

At Pragyan Paathshala, we believe every child is unique and deserves an opportunity to learn, explore and grow at their own pace. Our teaching-learning approach focuses on making education engaging, practical, interactive and meaningful.

We integrate child-centered teaching methods, creative activities, collaborative learning, technology and diverse learning resources to make the learning process more effective and enjoyable.

By recognizing students' interests, abilities and individual strengths, we aim to create learning experiences that encourage confidence, independence, curiosity and lifelong learning.

Our commitment goes beyond academic achievement. We focus on nurturing well-rounded individuals equipped with knowledge, values, practical skills and a positive attitude toward life.
